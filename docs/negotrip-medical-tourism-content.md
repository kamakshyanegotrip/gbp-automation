# Negotrip — medical tourism content pack

Everything below goes on the **existing verified Negotrip profile**. No new
listing, no verification, no duplicate risk. Written to Google's field limits.

**One formatting rule:** Google Business Profile fields are plain text. No `#`,
no `**`, no `-` bullets — they render as literal characters. (This is what is
currently wrong with the live description; the replacement below is clean.)

## Business description

Replaces the current one. Plain text, blank line between paragraphs.

```
Negotrip is a Bhubaneswar-based travel company, arranging medical travel, pilgrimage and leisure tours across Odisha and India since 2017.

For patients coming to Bhubaneswar for treatment we coordinate the whole journey: choosing between hospitals and specialists, treatment estimates in writing before you commit, medical visa documentation, airport transfer, an escort through admission, accommodation with room for an attendant, and follow-up after discharge.

We are a facilitator, not a healthcare provider. We do not diagnose and we do not advise on treatment; those decisions rest with you and your doctors. What we remove is the logistical weight around them.

We also run Odisha tour packages, temple circuits and wellness retreats.
```

**That last paragraph matters** — it keeps the description honest about the
whole business rather than repositioning a travel agency as a medical operation.

**Why the facilitator disclaimer stays:** it is what makes the Travel agency
category defensible rather than a misrepresentation of a healthcare business.
Google applies extra scrutiny to anything health-adjacent, and this paragraph
answers the question before it is asked.

## Categories

**Keep Travel agency as primary.** It is verified, established and accurate.
Changing a primary category on a profile that already carries a policy
restriction invites a re-review you do not want.

**Add as secondary:** Tour operator.

**Do not add any medical category.** Medical clinic, medical centre, medical spa
and similar describe healthcare *providers*. You facilitate travel. That is
misrepresentation under Google's category policy, and health categories get the
closest scrutiny of any vertical.

The medical specialism is expressed through service items and the description —
which is exactly what those fields are for.

## Service items

Keep the existing Medical Tours and Wellness Tours; these sharpen and extend
them. Each description is within Google's 300-character limit.

**Medical Tourism Facilitation**
> End-to-end coordination for patients travelling to Bhubaneswar for treatment — hospital selection, appointments, travel, accommodation and follow-up, arranged as one package.

**Hospital and Specialist Coordination**
> We help you compare hospitals and consultants in Bhubaneswar and across Odisha, obtain treatment estimates in writing, and book the appointments before you travel.

**Second Opinion Coordination**
> Send us your reports and we will circulate them to consultants here and return a written second opinion, so the decision to travel is an informed one.

**Medical Visa Assistance**
> Guidance on medical and attendant visa documentation, invitation letters from the treating hospital, and what to expect at immigration.

**Airport Transfer and Patient Escort**
> Met at Biju Patnaik International Airport, transferred to your accommodation or straight to the hospital, with an escort who stays with you through admission.

**Treatment Package Planning**
> A single written itinerary covering treatment dates, travel, accommodation and costs, so there are no surprises mid-stay.

**Post-Treatment Recovery Stays**
> Accommodation chosen for recovery rather than sightseeing — close to the hospital, quiet, with space for an attendant and catering that respects a prescribed diet.

**Attendant and Family Accommodation**
> Most patients do not travel alone. We arrange accommodation for the people who come with you, at the same standard and nearby.

**Wellness and Ayurveda Retreats**
> Panchakarma, yoga and Ayurvedic wellness programmes at established centres in Odisha, for recovery or as a standalone retreat.

**Odisha Tour Packages**
> Guided tours across Odisha — Konark, Puri, Chilika, the tribal belt and the Buddhist circuit — for leisure travellers and for patients' families during a long stay.

**Temple and Pilgrimage Circuits**
> Jagannath Temple, Lingaraj, the Golden Triangle and pilgrimage routes beyond Odisha, arranged with transport, guides and accommodation.
