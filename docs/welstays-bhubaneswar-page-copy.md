# welstays.in — Medical Travel page copy

**Purpose.** Right now welstays.in describes luxury villa rentals in Chandigarh,
Delhi, Goa, Kerala, Lonavala, Mussoorie, Nainital, Rishikesh, Shimla and Udaipur.
Nothing on the site mentions Bhubaneswar, Odisha or medical travel.

Google cross-checks a Business Profile against the business's website. A listing
for a Bhubaneswar medical-tourism facilitator pointing at a site that describes a
villa rental company in ten other cities is a mismatch, and mismatches are what
video verification rejects. This page closes that gap.

**Where it goes.** A top-level page at `welstays.in/medical-travel`, linked from
the main navigation — not buried in the blog. Google needs to find it easily.

**Two rules that matter more than the words:**

1. The address, phone number and business name on this page must match the
   Business Profile **character for character**. Not "approximately". Google
   compares them literally. Decide the format once and use it everywhere.
2. Keep "a unit of Negotrip Pvt Ltd" visible in the footer or contact block. It
   is what makes your Negotrip-era paperwork read as supporting evidence rather
   than as a contradiction.

## The opening section

### Why a hospitality company does this

Welstays has hosted over 12,000 guests across ten destinations in India. What we
learned running holiday homes turns out to matter even more in medical travel:
that a long stay away from home is judged by the small things — a clean room
ready on arrival, someone who answers the phone, a meal that suits a restricted
diet, a driver who knows the way to the hospital gate.

Medical travel carries all of that, with far more at stake. So we apply the same
standards to it.

Welstays is based in Bhubaneswar and operates as a unit of Negotrip Pvt Ltd, a
travel company working across Odisha since 2019.

## What we arrange

*(These should mirror the service items on the Business Profile exactly — same
names, same order. Consistency between site and profile is the point.)*

**Hospital and treatment coordination**
We help you choose between hospitals and specialists in Bhubaneswar and across
Odisha, share treatment estimates in writing before you commit, and book the
appointments.

**Second opinion coordination**
Before you travel, we can circulate your reports to consultants here and come
back with a written second view — so the decision to travel is an informed one.

**Medical visa assistance**
Guidance on medical visa and attendant visa documentation, invitation letters
from the treating hospital, and what to expect at immigration.

**Airport transfer and patient escort**
Meeting you at Biju Patnaik International Airport, transfer to your
accommodation or straight to the hospital, and an escort who stays with you
through admission.

**Recovery stays**
Accommodation chosen for the situation rather than the brochure — close to the
hospital, quiet, with room for an attendant, and catering that respects a
prescribed diet. Long-stay rates for treatments that run over weeks.

**Family accommodation**
Most patients do not travel alone. We arrange space for the people who come with
you, at the same standard.

**Follow-up and discharge support**
Coordinating review appointments, medication supply and the journey home.

## An honest note about what we are

Welstays is a facilitator, not a healthcare provider. We do not give medical
advice, do not diagnose, and do not recommend a course of treatment. Those
decisions rest with you and your doctors. What we do is remove the logistical
weight around them — so that the choice you make is about the medicine, not
about who will meet you at the airport.
