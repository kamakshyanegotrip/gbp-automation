Assessed 23 September 2026 against Google's video verification requirement:
a *"business permit, invoice, or utility bill that matches the name on your
Business Profile."*

## But it does not support the listing you were about to create

Two mismatches, both on the same certificate:

**1. Every address points away from Odisha.**

| Document | Jurisdiction |
|---|---|
| Udyam certificate | S.A.S. Nagar (Mohali), **Punjab** |
| Udyam certificate (2023 version) | Patiala, **Punjab** |
| FIO Homes rent agreement (2254, Ground Floor, Zirakpur) | **Punjab** |
| 2273 A affidavit | **Punjab** |
| MDB affidavit | **Punjab** |
| Agreement (Anita Nautiyal ↔ Negotrip Pvt Ltd) | **Uttarakhand**, Haridwar |

Not one document places the business in Bhubaneswar. A reviewer looking at a
Bhubaneswar listing and this paperwork sees a Punjab company.

**2. Every activity code is hospitality, not travel.**

The registered NIC codes are 55101 (hotels, motels, inns, resorts), 55109
(short-stay accommodation — holiday homes, guest houses), 56101 (restaurants)
and 56210 (event catering).

There is no travel agency code, no tour operator code, and nothing
health-related. A medical-tourism facilitation listing has no documentary
support at all — the certificate describes an accommodation business.

The four agreements are Punjab and Uttarakhand property rentals. They confirm a
real trading history, which is worth something, but they say nothing about
Bhubaneswar and nothing about facilitation.

## The fix is two amendments to one certificate

Both are free, online, and self-service at the official portal —
**udyamregistration.gov.in**, under "Update Udyam Registration", authenticated by
OTP to the registered mobile (7978272488). Ignore the sites charging ₹599 for
this; they are intermediaries reselling a free government service.

**Amendment 1 — add the Bhubaneswar address.** Udyam supports additional unit
addresses alongside the official address. Adding the Bhubaneswar office as a unit
makes the certificate match the Business Profile's address instead of
contradicting it.

**Amendment 2 — add NIC division 79.** That is *travel agency, tour operator and
other reservation service activities*: 7911 travel agency, 7912 tour operator,
7990 other reservation services. This is the code that makes a facilitation
listing defensible, and it is the honest description of what Welstays Bhubaneswar
would do.

After both, the certificate reads: **Negotrip Private Limited, unit Welstays, at
a Bhubaneswar address, registered for travel agency and tour operator
activities.** Show that at video verification and there is nothing left to argue
about.

Allow a few days for the amended certificate to regenerate before verifying.

## Revised sequence

1. **Udyam amendments** — address and NIC code. Free, online, a few days to
   regenerate. *Do this first; everything else waits on it.*
2. **Publish `welstays.in/medical-travel`** — copy already drafted. Address and
   phone must match the profile character for character.
3. **Create the Business Profile** — needs the Bhubaneswar address, a phone
   distinct from Negotrip's, and confirmation that patients are met in person.
4. **Verify** — only once 1 and 2 are done, with the amended Udyam certificate
   to hand.

Steps 1 and 2 run in parallel. Step 3 can happen any time, since an unverified
profile publishes nothing.
